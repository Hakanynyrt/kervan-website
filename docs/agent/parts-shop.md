## parts-shop (kiriciparca.com) — M1, open to search engines

- **Static, no router, no server code.** Every page is prerendered with its props embedded as JSON (`<script id="kv-page">`) and hydrated by `main.tsx`; links are plain `<a>`. Pages: `/`, `/kirici-ucu` (the search box with the make chips above and below it; since 2026-10-21 every model is listed before a search too, as picture cards 24 at a time with "Daha fazla göster" (`ModelTable all cards`, `PAGE` in `ModelSearch.tsx`; owner: "Kırıcı ucu sayfasında da tüm modeller listelensin"); earlier models were listed only once one was typed, `?q=` from the home search box; owner, 2026-10-16: "uç kısımında modeller gözükmüyor" put the makes back, and `/marka/<make>` lists every model of the make at once, the box only narrowing them, as cards (`Cards` in `ModelSearch.tsx`; owner, 2026-10-20; every card is in the prerendered HTML, those past the first 24 `hidden` until "Daha fazla göster" (`crawlAll`), so each model page has a plain link; `/kirici-ucu` renders only the shown page and reaches the models through the make chips): the model's own tip picture, or, with none of its own, the breaker page's sample (`sample` on `BreakerCard`: nearest-diameter pictured tip, same make within 15 mm, else any make within 30 mm) labelled "Temsili görsel" (owner, 2026-10-21: "tümünde de uçlar gözüksün"; it replaces the 2026-10-20 large-diameter tile), the large diameter only when no sample exists, then name, diameter and the net USD list price or its span over the model's tips with the TL amount at the CBRT rate under it (owner: "tl fiyatı da gözüksün") (`price` on `BreakerCard`, "+ vergiler" / "export price" next to it; "Fiyat için sorun" when none is priced; owner: "Fiyat da yazsın")), name and working diameter; owner, 2026-10-20: "modelleri fotoğrafları ile birlikte"). A model page shows its tip types as photo tiles under the title (a tile picks the type like the chips); a model whose own tips have no picture shows the nearest-diameter pictured tip of the same make within 15 mm, else of any make within 30 mm, labelled as a sample (owner, 2026-10-16: "seçilen modelin altında uçlar fotoğraflı gözüksün"). From the catalogue only: the no-space form plus merged catalogue spellings that differ by more than case, spaces, dashes or slashes, spellings with stray punctuation ("(61 , 62)", "90/ P") left out ("Diğer yazılışlar: Furukawa HB20G", `aliases`, `src/lib/model-aliases.ts`; when the series letters only repeat the make (MSB MS 200, MTB MT 275, Neuson NE 6) the make-plus-number form "MSB 200" is added too, from Search Console: people search "msb 200"; the full list also in `Product.alternateName`, but the meta description carries at most the no-space form and only while it stays ≤ 155 characters, `DESC_MAX` in `page-head.ts`), a facts block (tip size(s) Ø × length, tip types, the rough carrier class of the primary size only, the one the title and the product box use, prefixed "Ø135 mm:" when the model has several sizes, the other parts we make for it).
