## heat-treatment (kervanheat.com)

- Single-page site (served at `/` and `/en/`): `src/App.tsx` composes the sections in `src/components/` (Hero = the page opening, Services, TechnicalCapacity, Craft, About, Contact, Footer, …). No router, no 3D.
- `src/components/Hero.tsx` (`#top`, **the page opening**: first in `<main>`, directly under the header; copy in the dict's `hero`): H1 (`title1 title2`, Inter bold, no serif italic), one paragraph (`sub`), the one action "Teklif Al" (`#contact`) with the phone line under it (`ORG_PHONE` / `ORG_PHONE_E164` from `@kervan/seo`, label `contact.phoneLabel`), the four capacity stats, and **one** large photo with a caption (`hero.photo`; thin single frame, caption 15 px). Phones (< `lg`): H1, paragraph and the button come before the photo (the button is on the first 390×844 screen), stats follow the photo; `lg+`: copy column on the left (copy centred, stats at the bottom, level with the photo's caption), photo fills the right column's height. The photo is cut from our own plant photo `public/photos/uclar/uclar-pile.jpeg` (1536 px wide, so no larger source exists: crops are not upscaled): `/photos/isil-islem/hero-uc-parcalar-01-{1024,1536}.webp` (desktop, 1536×1250 crop) and `hero-uc-parcalar-telefon-01-{780,1170}.webp` (phones, 3:2) in a `<picture>`, WebP quality 82, eager + `fetchpriority=high` (written lowercase: React 18 warns on `fetchPriority`); a changed picture gets a new name (`-02`). Motion: only the photo's CSS `kv-hero-drift` (scale 1 → 1.04 over 40 s, one way, holds, no loop; off under reduced motion); no entrance animation, so the prerendered HTML is final. On the night theme the photo gets a 22 % dark overlay (`--kv-photo-dim`, 0 in the day block). The 3D cooling opening (three.js, `#sogutma`) was removed, with its media; `three` is no longer a heat dependency. The furnace-line film stays (next bullet).
- `src/components/HeroFilm.tsx` — the hero's framed furnace loop (`/videos/isil-islem/firin-hatti-loop-01*`; copy in the dict's `hero.film`). **Place:** a framed 16:9 card that `Hero.tsx` renders _below_ its text + photo + stats block (a separate wrapper after the `#top` section, so it is never in the first 390×844 screen and does not push the H1 or the "Teklif Al" button; on desktop it follows the photo and stats). It always uses the `-wide` poster/mp4 (the portrait files are unused now). Static mode renders only the poster `<picture>`; the `<video>` mounts client-side `startAt` seconds (default 1) after hydration, then on idle, on 4g without Save-Data, when the card is ≥ 25 % on screen, and starts at `START_AT` = the poster frame (re-extract both posters if that changes). Reduced motion = poster only, no button. A load/decode error (404, no H.264) falls back to the plain poster without the button. The pause button is required (WCAG 2.2.2). Any re-encode gets a new filename.
- "Tesisimiz" (`Craft.tsx`) shows the dict's `craft.photos` (`/photos/isil-islem/<base>-640/-1080.webp`, 4:5, four distinct shots). All hero/plant media is cut from our own `public/videos/atolye/furnace-01.mp4` (the other clips are breaker parts); a changed image gets a new filename (`-02`).
- Day theme: the light day palette everywhere; the header takes the page theme from the top (no `kv-night` band any more).
- `functions/_middleware.ts` — the day/night mark for pages (see `platform.md`); it passes `/api/*` and every non-HTML answer through untouched.
- **`functions/api/rfq.ts`** — a Cloudflare Pages Function. It is the RFQ endpoint for **both** sites (see below).
- Services (`Services.tsx`, `#hizmetler`): the process list from the dict's `services.processes` (owner, Oct 2026: sertleştirme (su verme), temperleme, gerilim giderme, normalizasyon, yumuşatma tavlaması), the quality line `services.quality` (hardness report for every batch), then two cards in a two-column grid ("Fason Isıl İşlem", "Üretim & Entegrasyon"; keep an even number so no empty cell shows). JSON-LD `Service` offers list the same processes. Capacity figures (Ø1200 mm, 2,5 t) are the owner's: do not change them.
- Header: the "Teklif Al" button shows at every width (compact on phones). The floating WhatsApp button (`WhatsAppFAB.tsx`, 48 px in the 12 px corner on phones) shows from the top on phones, after half a screen on wider ones; hidden over the footer and, on phones, over the form. WhatsApp / Maps links: `src/lib/contact.ts` (number from `ORG_PHONE_E164`, Maps = a plain search link for `ORG_STREET`, no embed).
- Legal name on this site (About heading, footer ©, KVKK, Organization JSON-LD): `ORG_REGISTERED_NAME` from `@kervan/seo`. Contact shows the street address (`ORG_STREET`) with a Google Maps link, phone, WhatsApp, e-mail and hours.
- Share images: `public/og-tr-02.png` / `og-en-02.png` (1200×630, KRV mark + text, dark ember; made offline from an HTML template with Playwright), `og:image` per language in `src/lib/seo.ts`; a new image gets a new name. `public/favicon.ico` (16 + 32 px PNGs in an ICO). 404: `dist/404.html` (tr) and `dist/en/404.html` (`lang="en"`, English first). `_headers` sends HSTS (`max-age=31536000; includeSubDomains`) on every answer (`/*`).
- The "Üretim & Entegrasyon" service card's last bullets link kervanbreaker.com ("Katalog") and kiriciparca.com ("Online mağaza") (`ServiceLink` bullets in the dict); the footer links both sister sites (same tab, no `rel`).
- `public/_redirects` 301s legacy breaker URLs (`/keski`, `/catalog.html`, `/products/*`, …) to kervanbreaker.com.
