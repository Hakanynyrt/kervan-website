## heat-treatment (kervanheat.com)

- Single-page site (served at `/` and `/en/`): `src/App.tsx` composes the sections in `src/components/` (Hero, Cooling, Services, TechnicalCapacity, Craft, About, Contact, Footer, …). No router; the only 3D is the Cooling section below.
- `src/components/HeroFilm.tsx` — the hero's framed furnace loop (`/videos/isil-islem/firin-hatti-loop-01*`). Static mode renders only the poster `<picture>`; the `<video>` mounts client-side after the hero sequence (`startAt = t0 + 2`, keep in sync with the stats stagger), on 4g without Save-Data, when the card is on screen, and starts at `START_AT` = the poster frame (re-extract both posters if that changes). Below `lg` it sits before the stats so phones see it on the first screen; a load/decode error (404, no H.264) falls back to the plain poster without the button. The pause button is required (WCAG 2.2.2). Any re-encode gets a new filename.
- `src/components/Cooling.tsx` (`#sogutma`, between Hero and Services; copy in the dict's `cooling`) — "Isınıp soğuyan parça": one breaker moil tip that, as the visitor scrolls through a 220vh section with a sticky stage, cools from furnace-hot (orange-yellow, point hottest) through cherry and dull red to glossy black steel. `CoolingScene.tsx` is the three.js part (`three` is a heat dependency, loaded only as a lazy chunk when the section is within one viewport): a `MeshStandardMaterial` with an `onBeforeCompile` heat term driven by one uniform `uTemp` = 1 − smoothstep(0.05, 0.9, progress) (blackbody ramp, axial gradient toward the point, fresnel edge, mottling; a dark oxide and rough finish blend into the black metal, no material swap); the axis comes from the decoded GLB positions at load. Cold look (owner): body `#20232a` metalness 0.85 roughness 0.34, machined faces `#2c3038` metalness 1 roughness 0.24, gain 1.6, environment 0.7 and key 2.6 when cold, rim light warm white → ember with heat, ACES. Restrained: no bloom, sparks or haze; the hero film stays. It draws only while on screen and the tab is visible (every frame while easing, ~30 fps for the idle roll drift), pixel ratio ≤ 1.5 on touch / 2 otherwise. Static mode (prerender/hydrate) and reduced motion render a normal-height section with the poster (`/photos/isil-islem/sogutma-uc-01-{640,1080}.webp`, the cherry-red frame, 3:2) and never touch WebGL; no WebGL, a chunk or model load error → the poster in the stage. Model: `public/models/isil-islem-uc-01.glb` (the quantized tip, geometry only; `/models/*` is immutable in `_headers`, so a changed model gets a new name). Numbers (temperatures) are not shown on the page.
- "Tesisimiz" (`Craft.tsx`) shows the dict's `craft.photos` (`/photos/isil-islem/<base>-640/-1080.webp`, 4:5, four distinct shots). All hero/plant media is cut from our own `public/videos/atolye/furnace-01.mp4` (the other clips are breaker parts); a changed image gets a new filename (`-02`).
- **`functions/api/rfq.ts`** — the only server-side code in the repo, a Cloudflare Pages Function. It is the RFQ endpoint for **both** sites (see below).
- The "Üretim & Entegrasyon" service card's last bullets link kervanbreaker.com ("Katalog") and kiriciparca.com ("Online mağaza") (`ServiceLink` bullets in the dict); the footer links both sister sites (same tab, no `rel`).
- `public/_redirects` 301s legacy breaker URLs (`/keski`, `/catalog.html`, `/products/*`, …) to kervanbreaker.com.
