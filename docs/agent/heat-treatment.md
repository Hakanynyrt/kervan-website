## heat-treatment (kervanheat.com)

- Single-page site (served at `/` and `/en/`): `src/App.tsx` composes the sections in `src/components/` (Cooling = the page opening, Hero, Services, TechnicalCapacity, Craft, About, Contact, Footer, …). No router; the only 3D is the Cooling opening below.
- `src/components/HeroFilm.tsx` — the hero's framed furnace loop (`/videos/isil-islem/firin-hatti-loop-01*`). Static mode renders only the poster `<picture>`; the `<video>` mounts client-side after the hero sequence (`startAt = t0 + 2`, keep in sync with the stats stagger), on 4g without Save-Data, when the card is on screen, and starts at `START_AT` = the poster frame (re-extract both posters if that changes). Below `lg` it sits before the stats so phones see it on the first screen; a load/decode error (404, no H.264) falls back to the plain poster without the button. The hero follows the opening, so its entrance sequence (and the film's arming, `hold` until then) starts when it scrolls into view (`useInView`, 25 %), delayed only by what is left of the intro curtain; its top padding no longer adds the sister strip (the header is already stuck there). The H1 is the hero's (the opening heading is an h2). The pause button is required (WCAG 2.2.2). Any re-encode gets a new filename.
- `src/components/Cooling.tsx` (`#sogutma`, **the page opening**: first in `<main>`, directly under the header, then the Hero; copy in the dict's `cooling`, scroll cue `cooling.scroll` "Kaydır") — "Isınıp soğuyan parça": one breaker moil tip that, as the visitor scrolls through a 220vh section (160vh below `md`) with a sticky stage, cools from furnace-hot (orange-yellow, point hottest) through cherry and dull red to glossy black steel. `CoolingScene.tsx` is the three.js part (`three` is a heat dependency, a lazy chunk mounted once the first paint has gone idle, `useClientIdle(2000)`): a `MeshStandardMaterial` with an `onBeforeCompile` heat term driven by one uniform `uTemp` = 1 − smoothstep(0.05, 0.9, progress) (blackbody ramp, axial gradient toward the point, fresnel edge, mottling; a dark oxide and rough finish blend into the black metal, no material swap); the axis comes from the decoded GLB positions at load. Cold look (owner): body `#20232a` metalness 0.85 roughness 0.34, machined faces `#2c3038` metalness 1 roughness 0.24, gain 1.6, environment 0.7 and key 2.6 when cold, rim light warm white → ember with heat, ACES. Restrained: no bloom, sparks or haze; the hero film stays. It draws only while on screen and the tab is visible (every frame while easing, ~30 fps for the idle roll drift), pixel ratio ≤ 1.5 on touch / 2 otherwise. First paint (animated) is the poster in the stage, framed like the scene and blended with `mix-blend-lighten` so its black backdrop disappears; the canvas fades in over it when its first frame is drawn. The poster `<img>` is eager + `fetchpriority=high` with one `sizes` in both modes, so the prerendered markup starts the LCP download. A faint "Kaydır ↓" cue (aria-hidden) fades in after the intro curtain and out once scrolling starts. Static mode (prerender/hydrate) and reduced motion render a normal-height block (top padding for the header) with the poster (`/photos/isil-islem/sogutma-uc-01-{640,1080}.webp`, the cherry-red frame, 3:2) and never touch WebGL; no WebGL, a chunk or model load error → the poster in the stage. Model: `public/models/isil-islem-uc-01.glb` (the quantized tip, geometry only; `/models/*` is immutable in `_headers`, so a changed model gets a new name). Numbers (temperatures) are not shown on the page.
- "Tesisimiz" (`Craft.tsx`) shows the dict's `craft.photos` (`/photos/isil-islem/<base>-640/-1080.webp`, 4:5, four distinct shots). All hero/plant media is cut from our own `public/videos/atolye/furnace-01.mp4` (the other clips are breaker parts); a changed image gets a new filename (`-02`).
- Day theme: the light day palette everywhere except `#sogutma` (`kv-night bg-bg`: the glowing-to-black tip only reads on dark, so the opening stays a dark band by day). While the opening is under the header (`#sogutma` bottom > 48 px), `Nav` adds `kv-night` to the header so its ink stays light on the band; past it the header takes the page theme again. The hero film is a framed photo card and works on both.
- `functions/_middleware.ts` — the day/night mark for pages (see `platform.md`); it passes `/api/*` and every non-HTML answer through untouched.
- **`functions/api/rfq.ts`** — a Cloudflare Pages Function. It is the RFQ endpoint for **both** sites (see below).
- The "Üretim & Entegrasyon" service card's last bullets link kervanbreaker.com ("Katalog") and kiriciparca.com ("Online mağaza") (`ServiceLink` bullets in the dict); the footer links both sister sites (same tab, no `rel`).
- `public/_redirects` 301s legacy breaker URLs (`/keski`, `/catalog.html`, `/products/*`, …) to kervanbreaker.com.
