## breaker-parts (kervanbreaker.com)

- React Router app: `src/pages/` (Home, Products, ProductDetail, Brands, Production, About, Contact, NotFound); sections in `src/components/` and `src/sections/`; product/brand data in `src/data/`.
- `public/_redirects` has **no** SPA fallback: every route is a prerendered file, unknown paths get `404.html` with HTTP 404. Heat-treatment URLs are 301'd to `https://kervanheat.com/`.
- `src/components/OpeningHold.tsx` — the empty first viewport over the 3D scene. It never fights scrolling: touch and the arrow/Home/End keys are native; only a mouse-wheel flick or Space/PageDown inside the opening glides to the hero (instant under reduced motion, with an instant-jump safety net if the glide is cancelled, never inside form fields). It unmounts only after scrolling has settled, cancelling any glide first, so a touch fling is not cut short and the page cannot land past the hero.
- `src/components/Scene.tsx` — Three.js background scene "Tek Parça" (owner-approved 2026-10-13, realism + pose update the same day): one black pointed (moil) breaker tip with two key slots standing steep like a studio product shot — long axis 65° off horizontal, point down and to the screen left, turned 25°, roll 95° so both key slots read as silhouette notches (camera fov 16°, distance 4.6, 3° below the tip). Every surface black, exactly as the approved prototype (night values below; day theme see the next bullets): forged body `#1d1f22` (metalness 0.55, roughness 0.36 ± 0.18 from a generated 128 px value noise used as roughness map and bump map, bump scale 0.3, UV-mapped through the model's own UVs, 3 tiles around × 5 along) and machined faces `#1e2024` (metalness 1, roughness 0.4). Do not replace the UV mapping with a shader mapping around the axis: the owner rejected that look. ACES at exposure 1.2. Lighting: a studio environment baked once into a PMREM (dark room, a narrow key strip, a cool right strip, a faint ember strip, a top box and an ember floor; it replaced `RoomEnvironment`), plus a warm key, a cool fill, an ember rim light (`#ff6a1a`, 3.5) circling behind it about every 12 s (start / reduced-motion frame at (2.2, 1.4, −2.6), as in the prototype) and a small ember bounce point light at the point. A faint additive ember pool lies on an unseen floor under the point (opacity 0.5 in the opening, 0.3 in the hero; keep it subtle, it must not read as a UI glow), and an ember radial glow sits behind the tip, low toward the point, as a CSS gradient on the container. No stars, no bloom, no post-processing. Model: `/kirici-ucu-siyah-02.glb` (~141 KB, plain `GLTFLoader` with `KHR_mesh_quantization`, no DRACO/meshopt; positions, normals and UVs (`TEXCOORD_0` as normalized uint16: u around 0..1, v along 0..1), node `kirici-ucu`, materials `body`/`machined`). Keep it local; a changed model gets a new file name. Scroll rail anchored on `[data-scene-pose="hero"]`; the tip is sized by its projected height: opening = centred, 80 % of the frame tall and slightly low so it clears the nav (portrait: 70 %, at most 80 % of the width), with a ±3.5° sway; into the hero it turns a further −10°, shrinks to 42 % and stands above the hero figures in the right column (point on their top rule); on portrait it shrinks to 34 % and stands right of the heading lines; past the hero it fades out. Once `OpeningHold` has unmounted the tip keeps the hero pose. It draws on demand (every frame while moving, ~30 fps idle, nothing while hidden or faded out), caps the pixel ratio at 1.5 on touch devices (2 elsewhere), follows `prefers-reduced-motion` live (static approved frame), and leaves an ember glow when WebGL is unavailable. The owner's hidden entry (the mint key star, 28 px hit area, `onSecret`) circles behind the tip. Both three.js users (`Scene` on Home, `ExportsGlobe` inside `Exports`) are `React.lazy` chunks, the globe only mounts about a screen before its section, so the main bundle stays small and other routes never download three.js. The globe still loads `/kirici-uc.glb` (symlink to the root `public/`) and draws only while on screen.
- Links to the shop kiriciparca.com (`src/lib/shop.ts`: `shopUrl`, `SHOP_PATH` product slug → shop page; `components/ShopLink.tsx`; copy in the dict's `shop` block): a "Mağaza ↗" menu entry (desktop from `lg`, and the mobile menu), a line under the quote button on product pages (no link for `saplama`, the adapter-plate stud the shop does not sell), one line under the products grid, the compatibility page and the 404, an "Online mağaza" row on the contact page and the footer, which also links kervanheat.com. The footer's social icons come from `ORG_SOCIALS` (`@kervan/seo`, labels in the dict's `footer.social`) and every public email shown is `ORG_PUBLIC_EMAIL`; never hard-code either. Sister-site links open in the same tab with no `rel`, like `SisterSiteStrip`, and never promise prices (only the owner's priced parts show one). The global `a { color: inherit; text-decoration: none }` rule in `globals.css` (both apps) is unlayered and outranks Tailwind's colour/underline utilities on an `<a>`, so style a link's text on an inner `<span>`.
- Day theme: the whole home, opening and hero included, is the paper day palette (owner 2026-10-13, "Gündüz giriş de açık olsun"; the dark stage band of option A is gone). `Scene` follows the theme live (`lib/day-theme.ts`: `isDayTheme` / `onThemeChange`, so the Tema select or a `data-sun` change relights it without reload) and by day keeps the same tip, model, pose, layout and motion but lights it as black steel on a light seamless studio sweep: its own PMREM (`DAY` in `Scene.tsx`: mid-dark room so the steel stays black, bright floor and back wall, a stronger key strip, top box and right strip, black flags at the sides), exposure 1.0, key 1.4, fill 0.35, a soft warm rim (`#ffb48a`, 0.8) instead of the ember one, no ember bounce, a soft grey contact + cast shadow under the point instead of the additive ember pool (pink stain on paper), a pale white backdrop light (CSS) instead of the ember glow, and the key star drawn darker mint (`#14866a`, normal blending, 0.55 opacity; additive mint showed as a stray green dot) with the same path, 28 px hit area and `onSecret`. On portrait screens by day the tip fades out as the hero arrives (no dark stage to hide it behind the heading lines). No-WebGL fallback: a pale studio light by day, the ember glow at night. `html.opening-active body` is black only at night. Night is the approved look, unchanged. Below the hero `ExportsGlobe` turns into a paper globe with ink borders (follows the theme select live), the brand-logo tiles darken their cream wordmarks with a CSS filter, product placeholders use `bg-warm`/`bg-soft` tokens.
- Its Pages Functions are the owner-only `api/tech/*` endpoints (see "Owner-only Teknik Bilgiler") and `functions/_middleware.ts`, the day/night mark for pages (see `platform.md`; it passes `/api/*` and every non-HTML answer through untouched). The contact form still posts cross-origin to `https://kervanheat.com/api/rfq` in production (`/api/rfq` in dev, which 404s). `smoke.sh` checks `/api/tech/content` returns JSON 401, not HTML.

## Private VEGA tip catalog (breaker-parts, KV)

- The owner-only tip catalog (609 rows, ~420 KB) is **not** in the repo or the bundle and is too big for a Pages secret (5 KB limit). It lives in a Workers KV namespace bound to the `kervan-breaker-parts` Pages project as **`VEGA_CATALOG`** (Production only), key `catalog:v1`.
- `GET /api/tech/catalog` (`functions/api/tech/catalog.ts`) returns it only for a valid owner session (same host + cookie checks as `/api/tech/content`). No session → JSON 401 before KV is read; no binding → 503; empty key → 404. `smoke.sh` checks the anonymous 401 only.
- Update the data without a deploy: `node scripts/vega-import.mjs <catalog.csv> <out.json>` (writes **outside** the repo, refuses paths inside it), then upload `out.json` to KV key `catalog:v1` (dashboard: KV → namespace → Add entry → Upload file). Never commit the CSV or JSON (`*.csv` and `vega-catalog*.json` are gitignored). Keep the namespace unbound in Preview.
- Optional KV key `popular:v1` in the same namespace: `{ schema: 1, models: { "<catalog model>": 1 | 2 }, aliases?: { "<breaker name>": "<catalog model>" } }` (1 = sells most in Turkey, 2 = sells well). The endpoint returns it next to the catalog; missing/malformed ⇒ no badges, catalog unaffected. It is business data: never commit it — the owner pastes it in the dashboard (KV → namespace → `popular:v1`).
- Technical drawings (the side-view drawing with dimensions under each model in the VEGA PDF) live in the same namespace as one value, key `drawings:v1` (~2.4 MB). Build it with `node scripts/vega-drawings.mjs <catalogue.pdf> <catalog.json> <out.json>` (needs `pdftohtml` + ImageMagick `convert`; writes **outside** the repo; exits non-zero if any row has no drawing). The PDF stores the images out of order, so the script pairs them by position and part numbers, never by file order. `GET /api/tech/drawings` serves it under the same session rules as the catalog (401 before KV, 503 without the binding, 404 when empty); the page fetches it once when the first detail panel opens and shows nothing when it is missing. `smoke.sh` checks the anonymous 401 only. Never commit the PDF, images or JSON.
- Drawing-derived features: each catalog row may carry `rearStep` (boolean: the rear end is a narrower stub with a shoulder), `slotEnd` (`'rounded' | 'tapered'`: the key slot ends in a short radius or a long conical ramp) and `tipAngleDeg` (the number printed on the tip view, e.g. 45). They are read from the drawings (two independent vision passes plus a tie-break pass; `rearStep` is cross-checked against the table's rear diameter, `tipAngleDeg` against the catalogue text) and merged with `node scripts/vega-attrs.mjs <catalog.json> <attrs.json> <out.json>` (outside the repo; the result replaces `catalog:v1`). Missing/`null` means unknown, and the catalog tab never hides a tip over an unknown value. The slope of the rear shoulder is **not** offered: the drawings are too small to tell a chamfered shoulder from a square one (the passes agreed on only 71 % of rows).
- Owner-only reading guide: optional KV key `guide:v1` (`{ schema: 1, breakage: { intro, zones[], defectVsMisuse, claimChecklist, disclaimer } }`, TR+EN) is returned as `guide` by `/api/tech/catalog` and shown as a collapsible panel "where a tip breaks / what warranty covers" in the catalog tab. It is industry-norm research, not Kervan's own terms; Kervan's warranty terms are the owner's decision and, if added, belong in this KV key, never in the repo. Missing/malformed ⇒ no panel, catalog unaffected.
- Usage guide and carrier weight (public, generic knowledge, in the repo): `lib/vega-usage.ts` maps the tip diameter to a rough carrier tonnage band (union of manufacturer/dealer data points listed in the file; none below Ø75 or above Ø209) and the dict holds a per-tip-type guide (rock, where, watch-out). Both are labelled as approximate; the breaker maker's own tip recommendation wins.
- Local test: `wrangler kv key put --namespace-id=VEGA_CATALOG --local "catalog:v1" --path <catalog.json>`, then `wrangler pages dev dist --kv VEGA_CATALOG --compatibility-date=2026-04-17` with test secrets in `.dev.vars`.
